## Style Direction

**industrial x iron-brass** -- Condensed uppercase display type, dark iron hero, warm brass accent. Same palette family as Discount Collision Repair (batch sibling), but differentiated via section ordering, hero composition, copy angles, and Indianapolis-specific content.

### Differentiation from Discount Collision Repair (batch sibling)

Both use industrial x iron-brass, but this build MUST feel distinct:

1. **Hero:** Wide shop-floor overhead shot (technician leaning into engine bay) vs Discount's spray-booth close-up
2. **Section order:** Process-first (how it works immediately after hero) vs Discount's reviews-first
3. **Services layout:** Alternating left/right blocks with photo accents vs Discount's card grid
4. **Process:** Vertical numbered steps vs Discount's horizontal timeline
5. **Copy angle:** "Perfect 5.0 from 36 reviews" lean + Indianapolis winter-damage specialization vs Discount's Amarillo/discount-value angle
6. **Unique feature:** Indiana winter weather expertise woven into copy (salt damage, freeze-thaw, I-465 corridor)
7. **Gallery:** Before/after narrative vs Discount's process-organized

### Why this pairing

- The `auto-body` niche maps to `industrial x iron-brass` per niche-defaults
- Oswald condensed + iron bg + brass accent reads "trade shop" at a glance
- For an Indianapolis IN collision shop, the palette communicates reliability and working-class competence

## Tone of Voice

Direct, no-nonsense, quality-focused. This shop has a perfect 5.0 rating with 36 reviews in Indianapolis. The copy leans into that specific proof point and the reality of Midwest driving: harsh winters, road salt corrosion, I-465 and I-70 interchange traffic. Short declarative sentences. The customer is stressed after an accident, so give them the path forward immediately.

## References used

### 1. Crash Champions (https://www.crashchampions.com)
- Hero composition: Location-detection utility hero with toll-free number, service access prioritized
- Section order: Trust messaging ("We Believe The Difference is Trust") precedes service details
- Copy voice: Direct, people-first. Short punchy statements. Avoids jargon.
- Photo treatment: Warm documentary, real team members, natural lighting
- Trust signals: Lifetime guarantee, founder-led, Great Place to Work Certified, insurance compatibility, manufacturer certifications
- Conversion path: "Book an Appointment" persistent CTA in nav, footer, mid-page
- **Apply to this build:** (1) Lifetime warranty as standalone visual block mid-page, (2) persistent phone CTA in nav and mobile sticky bar, (3) insurance compatibility strip as a dedicated trust section, (4) process-first section ordering after hero to guide stressed customers

### 2. Kinfolk Magazine (https://kinfolk.com) -- non-competitor editorial reference
- Hero composition: Minimalist cover with clean typographic hierarchy
- Section order: Featured content cards leading to category exploration
- Copy voice: Sophisticated brevity, descriptive subtitles, intellectual authority through deliberate phrasing
- Photo treatment: Minimalist, substantial whitespace, monochromatic and curated
- Trust signals: Named contributors, institutional affiliations
- Conversion path: Subscription tiers presented early and repeatedly
- **Apply to this build:** (1) Generous whitespace between sections for visual breathing room, (2) editorial brevity in copy (shorter paragraphs, more line breaks), (3) clean typographic hierarchy with intentional scale contrast between heading levels, (4) photo treatment with deliberate negative space around images

### Cross-reference design thesis
Lead with process clarity for stressed post-accident customers (per Crash Champions' trust-first approach). Apply Kinfolk's editorial spacing to keep the industrial palette from feeling claustrophobic. Process steps immediately after hero, then reviews, then services. Indianapolis winter-damage expertise woven throughout as the local-specific differentiator. Warranty block as a visual break point between proof sections.
